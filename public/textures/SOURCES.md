# Texture provenance

All textures are CC0 from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), downscaled to 1024px and converted to WebP.

- rock-diff.webp, rock-nor.webp, rock-disp.webp, rock-ao.webp: "dark_rock" (https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_rock), diffuse, normal (GL), displacement, AO.
- cracks-ao.webp, cracks-disp.webp: "dry_ground_01" (https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_01), AO and displacement, used as the fissure mask for the magma.
